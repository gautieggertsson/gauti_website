title: "Replication Files"
description: "Code and data for replicating published results."
---

Code and data for each paper that has a replication package, newest first. Packages deposited with a journal are linked at the journal's archive: openICPSR for the AEA journals, Zenodo for the *Review of Economic Studies*, and the Society for Economic Dynamics archive for the *Review of Economic Dynamics*.

- [Liquidity Traps: A Unified Theory of the Great Depression and the Great Recession](/research/a-unified-theory-of-the-great-depression-and-the-great-recession/) (*Journal of Economic Literature*, 2025) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E237322V1)

- [Negative Nominal Interest Rates and the Bank Lending Channel](/research/negative-nominal-interest-rates-and-the-bank-lending-channel/) (*Review of Economic Studies*, 2024) — [Data and code, Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8113824)

- [The Slanted-L Phillips Curve](/research/the-slanted-l-phillips-curve/) (*AEA Papers and Proceedings*, 2024) — [Data appendix, NBER](https://data.nber.org/data-appendix/w32172/)

- [Time Consistency and the Duration of Government Debt: A Model of Quantitative Easing](/research/time-consistency-and-the-duration-of-government-debt-a-model-of-quantitative-easing/) (*Review of Economic Studies*, 2023) — [Data and code, Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7007875)

- [A Toolkit for Solving Models with a Lower Bound on Interest Rates of Stochastic Duration](/research/a-toolkit-for-solving-models-with-a-lower-bound-on-interest-rates-of-stochastic-duration/) (*Review of Economic Dynamics*, 2021) — [Code on GitHub](https://github.com/gautieggertsson/2-state-toolkit) (maintained version) · [Archived zip](2-state-toolkit-master.zip)

- [Dynamic Debt Deleveraging and Optimal Monetary Policy](/research/dynamic-debt-deleveraging-and-optimal-monetary-policy/) (*American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics*, 2020) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E231504V1)

- [Aging, Output Per Capita and Secular Stagnation](/research/aging-output-per-capita-and-secular-stagnation/) (*American Economic Review: Insights*, 2019) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E231432V1)

- [Log-linear Approximation versus an Exact Solution at the ZLB in the New Keynesian Model](/research/log-linear-approximation-versus-an-exact-solution-at-the-zlb-in-the-new-keynesian-model/) (*Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control*, 2019) — [Replication code](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/wl1qbc3y7z6pz48s53q2o/ReplicationCodes_EggertssonSingh.zip?rlkey=tddo815baab9w3wd3c581esh7&st=9v2e3ku3&dl=0) (hosted by Sanjay Singh)

- [A Model of Secular Stagnation: Theory and Quantitative Evaluation](/research/a-model-of-secular-stagnation-theory-and-quantitative/) (*American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics*, 2019) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E114159V1)

- [Sticky Prices versus Sticky Information: Does it Matter for Policy Paradoxes?](/research/sticky-prices-versus-sticky-information-does-it-matter-for-policy-paradoxes/) (*Review of Economic Dynamics*, 2019) — [Data and code, RED archive](https://ideas.repec.org/c/red/ccodes/18-259.html)

- [The Great Escape? A Quantitative Evaluation of the Fed's Liquidity Facilities](/research/the-great-escape-a-quantitative-evaluation-of-the-feds-liquidity-facilities/) (*American Economic Review*, 2017) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E112902V1)

- [Was the New Deal Contractionary?](/research/was-the-new-deal-contractionary/) (*American Economic Review*, 2012) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E112501V1)

- [Great Expectations and the End of the Depression](/research/great-expectations-and-the-end-of-the-depression/) (*American Economic Review*, 2008) — [Data and code, openICPSR](https://doi.org/10.3886/E113257V1)

Other published papers are theoretical or predate journal replication archives and have no separate package; working-paper versions and appendices are on each paper's page.
